Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about working with a smart home company.

What does a smart home company do?

A smart home company plans, installs, and supports connected technology in your house, including lighting, thermostats, security, audio, and the controls that tie them together. At OM Wave LLC, we handle the design and setup so the system works smoothly from day one.

What is the difference between home automation and smart home integration?

Home automation means your devices act on their own through schedules, sensors, or scenes. Smart home integration means connecting different devices and brands so they share one app or control point. Most homes benefit from both.

Can you add smart home systems to a home I already live in?

Yes. Many of our projects are upgrades to existing homes. We look at what you already have, recommend what makes sense, and install it with as little disruption as possible.

Do I need to replace all my current devices?

Usually not. We check which of your current devices can be connected and only recommend replacements where it improves reliability or ease of use.
